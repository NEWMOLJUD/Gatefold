# Code of Conduct

Gatefold follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that address applies to this project; this page summarises it.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the whole community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or suffer unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers of the repository, through their GitHub profiles. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that go against this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
